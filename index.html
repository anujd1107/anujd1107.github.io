Happy Chocolate Day! 🍫

They say life is like a box of chocolates, but honestly, my life was just a plain wrapper until you came along. I know lately, I’ve been serving you a lot of "Dark Chocolate"—moments that were bitter, moods that were hard to handle, and mistakes that left a bad taste.

I admit I am not perfect. Sometimes I get stuck in my own head, and I forget to add the sugar that our relationship deserves. But I want you to know one thing: You are the only sweetness I need.

"Mana ki thodi kadwahat aa gayi hai baton mein,
Par yaqeen kar, mithaas sirf tere hone se hai.
Tu naraz bhi ho toh manzoor hai mujhe,
Kyunki meri zindagi ka har flavor sirf tujhse hai."

Just like chocolate creates a rush of happiness in the brain, you are the signal that makes my heart beat properly. Without you, everything feels bland.

I promise to try harder to be the "Milk Chocolate" in your life—soft, comforting, and there to make your bad days better.

"KitKat ki tarah break le lete hai gusse se,
Dairy Milk ki tarah ghul jaate hai ishq mein.
Aaj promise karta hoon tujhse,
Mithaas hi mithaas bhar dunga tere har ek risk mein."

- Yours, Anuj.
