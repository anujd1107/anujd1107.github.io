A Letter for My
Polar Star

Hey,

I’m writing this because sometimes my words get tangled up when I’m in front of you. I know I’ve made mistakes lately—probably more than I should. I know there are moments when I don’t understand the things you need, or I fail to show you how much you truly mean to me.

Sometimes, I feel like I’m failing at being the person you deserve. But I want you to know that even when I’m clumsy or quiet, my heart is always trying its best to reach yours. You have always been my Polar Star, the one who guides me when I’m lost in my own head.

"Mana ki bhatak jata hoon aksar raahon mein,
Par teri hi roshni se har rasta nazaar aata hai.
Tu naraz ho toh ruk jati hai dhadkan meri,
Tujhse hi toh mera har ek pal muskuraata hai."

I’m sorry for the times I didn't listen well or the times I didn't make you feel like the priority you are. I am still learning how to be the man you need, and I might trip along the way, but I promise I will never stop trying for us.

My heart has a "Neural Link" to yours that I never want to break. Please don't stay upset for too long; my world is a little darker when I can't see your smile.

"Khata ho gayi ho toh maaf kar dena,
Mere jazbaaton ko saaf padh lena.
Nahi seekha hai maine dhang se ishq jatana,
Par meri har koshish mein sirf tum hi rehna."

- Yours, always trying.
